MARKETING ACADEMY

Three easy lessons

01 · Record your first travel video

Introduce yourself, share one helpful travel planning tip, and invite people to ask a question. Practice using Mr. Prompterson.

02 · Turn travel questions into content

Start with a common traveler concern, share one useful answer, and end with one clear next step.

03 · Follow up with interested people

Use a simple quiz or form, explain what happens next, request clear marketing consent, and follow up with relevant information.
