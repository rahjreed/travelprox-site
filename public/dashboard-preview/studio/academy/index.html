LESSON 2 · Teach one useful thing

Education with curiosity

Answer part of the question with practical information. Leave a natural reason to explore further; never withhold essential terms or costs.

Example

Different ships cater to different travel styles, from family activities to quiet relaxation.

A travel business requires marketing, customer service and consistent follow-up, not just a love of travel.

Your mission

Write two useful sentences that follow your hook.

LESSON 3 · Invite the next step

Recommend

Recommend a relevant quiz. Explain what it helps someone discover, and make participation voluntary.

Example

Take my travel quiz to explore which cruise style fits you.

Take my business opportunity quiz to explore whether this type of business fits your interests.

Your mission

Write one clear, low-pressure quiz invitation.

Record and publish

Example

LESSON 5 · Follow up with permission

Build your list

Offer useful quiz results, request consent before marketing follow-up, and respect opt-outs. Track customer and recruiting interests separately. Explain business costs, risks and effort accurately; never promise earnings.

Example

Ask whether the traveler wants help comparing options.

Ask whether the prospect wants factual details about the business model and costs.
